# Master prompt: shift.AI authentication

Design and implement a new premium desktop sign-in and sign-up experience for shift.AI, using its existing orange, charcoal, warm neutral, and white palette. Make the account form the clearest action on the page.

Use a consistent full-width header: a compact brand at the left, an adjacent back-to-home link, and language selection at the right. Below it, compose two balanced columns. The left is an editorial cream canvas with generous space, a concise headline, and an original architectural stepped illustration representing progress from a question to a decision. Use flat, precise geometry instead of floating cards, charts, orbit animations, or an oversized dark marketing block. The right is a restrained form directly on white, with a small section label, a clear title, and a visible link to the other account flow.

Keep typography crisp, line lengths short, and controls consistent and comfortable, around 48px tall on full-height displays. Use orange for the primary action and illustration, charcoal for text, and subtle borders for inputs. Back navigation must remain anchored in the header. Fit sign-in, signup, and password recovery inside one desktop viewport so no page scrolling is required; scale spacing with viewport height instead of relying on scroll. Collapse to a single form column on narrow screens without hiding navigation.

Preserve real email/password authentication and configured Google sign-in. Keep a Continue with Google action visible below the primary submit button, disabled with a short explanation until a client ID is configured. Provide explicit labels, correct autocomplete, accessible field errors, focus the first invalid field, show/hide passwords, and prevent duplicate submissions. Explain the actual 12-character signup requirement before submission. Place the link to the other account flow at the top of the form panel. Never imply a successful login before verifying the session. Remove the local-access link, require account login by default, and replace the landing header's Open workspace action with Sign up. Ship password reset as a working flow with single-use, expiring, hashed tokens rather than a placeholder link. Do not add fake testimonials, unsupported privacy promises, or a new color theme.

Validate the real signup/login flows, invalid credentials, keyboard navigation, navigation links, desktop layout, and narrow-screen overflow. Keep verification servers and build output separate from normal development sessions.
